# Draconium

**Draconium** is the base metal of the mod: nearly every Draconic Evolution recipe uses it somewhere, directly or through a [Core](../getting_started/cores.md).

It comes as ore, **Dust**, **Nuggets**, **Ingots** and **Blocks**. Ore drops dust, and dust smelts 1:1 into ingots. Nine nuggets make an ingot and nine ingots make a block, and both go back the other way.

## Where to find it

Draconium ore generates in three dimensions:
- **Overworld**: deep down at Y -64 to -48, in roughly one chunk out of ten, as small veins in stone or deepslate.
- **Nether**: Y 4 to 16, about one chunk in thirty, but in big veins.
- **End**: three small veins in every chunk, Y 0 to 70, on the main island and the outer islands.

On this server Overworld ore is rare. **The End is the main source**, so plan your Draconium trips there.

## Mining

<ItemImage id="draconicevolution:end_draconium_ore" scale="2" />

Mine any of the ores with a pickaxe. Each one drops 2 to 4 **Draconium Dust**, and Fortune adds up to one more per level.

Silk Touch gives the ore block, which smelts into only one ingot, so Fortune is the better choice.

## Other sources

Killing the **Ender Dragon** also drops a large pile of Draconium Dust (about 64 by default), and a **Dragon Heart** for [Awakened Draconium](../getting_started/awakened_draconium.md).

On this server, **Mystical Agriculture** can also turn Draconium Essence into ingots; check JEI.

A **Draconium Block** resists the Wither and the Ender Dragon, and next to an enchanting table it counts as four bookshelves.
