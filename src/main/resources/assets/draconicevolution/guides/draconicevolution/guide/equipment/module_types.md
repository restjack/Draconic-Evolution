# Module Types

## Any powered item

- **Energy**: more OP storage.

- **Energy Link** (Wireless Energy Link, Quantum Energy Tunnel, Chaos-Entangled tunnel): before installing it, shift-right-click an active [Energy Core](../energy_core/overview.md) with it. The item then charges itself from that core. The Wyvern link works in the same dimension only; Draconic and Chaotic work across dimensions. Opening the link costs a lot of energy and keeping it open costs a little.

## Tools and weapons

- **Speed**: faster mining; on bows, faster draw.
- **AOE**: mine, till or hit an area, 3x3 up to 11x11 by tier. One per item.
- **Damage**: more melee damage.
- **Tree Harvest** (axe, staff): fells whole trees.
- **Selective Incineration**: destroys drops you mark as junk.
- **Ender Collection**: sends drops to your ender chest.

## Bows

- Projectile modules change arrow velocity, accuracy, gravity drop, penetration and base damage.
- **Auto Fire**: looses the arrow as soon as it is fully drawn.
- **Projectile Immunity Cancellation**: arrows hurt mobs that normally ignore them, such as endermen, the Wither and the Ender Dragon.

## Chestpiece

- Shield Control, Shield Capacity, Large Shield Capacity and Shield Recovery: see [Modular Armour](../equipment/modular_armour.md).
- Undying, Flight, Auto Feed, Night Vision, Jump, Hill Step, Aqua Adept, Mining Stabilizer and Speed.

Every module's tooltip lists its exact numbers, its size and its install limit.
