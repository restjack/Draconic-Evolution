# Optional Extras and Their Switches

Everything this port adds beyond bug fixes can be switched off in **config/brandon3055/DraconicEvolution.cfg**, section **Extras**. Each half has a master switch called **extras**; turning it off gives the official mod's behaviour.

Bug fixes are not in this list and are always on.

## Client (your own)

- **coreScreenDetails**: In/Out/Net lines, time to full/empty, short numbers.
- **coreGraph**: the graph button.
- **coreUnitToggle**: the OP/RF button.
- **structureHints**: the blocks-wrong line.
- **pylonModeColours**: green/red pylon particles.

## Server (everyone)

- **grinderXpPiping**: liquid XP out of the Mob Grinder.
- **guideBookRecipe**: this book's recipe.
- **coreRateHistory**: cores record their graph data.
- **pylonModeMessage**: the pylon INPUT/OUTPUT message.
- **coreMonitor**: the Core Monitor block.
- **beamForceField**: stabilizer beams push and hurt (plus **beamDamage**, **beamPush**, **beamAffectsPlayers**, **beamAffectsMobs**).

The server's choices reach your game when you join.

## Elsewhere

- Jade's own settings can turn off the Draconic Energy Core tooltips.
- The HD texture packs are off until you pick them in Resource Packs.
- The eye button and **blockEffectRenderDistance** control the core effects.
