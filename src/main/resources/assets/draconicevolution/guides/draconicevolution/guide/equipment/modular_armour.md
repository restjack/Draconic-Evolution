# Modular Armour

Draconic armour is a single **Chestpiece** in Wyvern, Draconic and Chaotic tiers. On its own it is a diamond-grade chestplate; its strength comes from [modules](../equipment/modules.md), above all the energy shield. If Curios is installed it can also be worn in a Curios slot.

Its grid holds most body modules: shield, Undying, Flight, Speed, Jump, Auto Feed, Night Vision and more.

## Energy shield

Install a **Shield Control** module to get a shield (one per chestpiece). Add **Shield Capacity** modules for more shield points, **Large Shield Capacity** for many points in one 2x2 module, and **Shield Recovery** for faster recharge.

The shield soaks up damage before your health. After a hit it waits out a cooldown (shorter on higher tier controllers), then recharges using energy.

## Shield upkeep

Holding the shield up also costs a little energy, more the bigger it is. If the chestpiece runs dry, the shield fades away over about a minute. Keep plenty of energy modules, or an Energy Link, in the same grid.

## Undying

When a fatal hit lands and the module is charged, **Undying** cancels the death, heals you, adds a short shield boost and gives a moment of invulnerability. Draconic and Chaotic modules also clear harmful effects.

It then recharges from the chestpiece: 2 minutes and 5M OP (Wyvern), 1 minute and 10M OP (Draconic), 45 seconds and 20M OP (Chaotic).

## Flight

The Wyvern **Flight** module gives elytra flight. Draconic and Chaotic also give creative-style flight, unless the server turned that off. A Toggle Flight key can be bound in Controls.

Chestpieces are fusion crafted from a diamond chestplate up through each tier.
