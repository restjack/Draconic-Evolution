# Celestial Manipulator

Controls the weather and the time of day. It needs power and holds 8 million OP.

**Weather**:
- Stop rain or start rain: 256,000 OP
- Start a thunderstorm: 384,000 OP

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:celestial_manipulator" />

**Time**: skip to sunrise, midday, sunset, moonrise, midnight or moonset, or skip a full day. Time is wound forward quickly, not jumped, at 320 OP per tick of time, so a full day costs about 7.7 million OP. The stop button ends it early.

## Redstone

The redstone tab picks which action a redstone signal starts. In **Skip 24** mode, turning the signal off stops the time skip early.

It only changes the clock; machines and crops do not get extra ticks.

The recipe uses a **Dragon Egg** and a Wyvern Core.
