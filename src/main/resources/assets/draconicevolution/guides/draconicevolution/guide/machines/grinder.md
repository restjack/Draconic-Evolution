# Mob Grinder

The **Mob Grinder** kills mobs in front of it using energy. When placed it faces you; the kill zone is in front of its face. At the largest setting the zone is a 7x7x7 cube; the screen lets you shrink it and show its outline.

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:machines/grinder" />

Each kill costs 80 OP per point of the mob's health (at least 400 OP). The grinder holds 1 million OP and only runs with at least 4,000 OP stored. Power it with cables or with a charged item in its energy slot.

## Features

- **Weapon slot**: a weapon placed here is used for the kills, so its enchantments apply. It loses durability but never breaks.
- **Items**: drops are pushed into any inventory touching the grinder.
- **Experience**: orbs are stored, up to 8,192 points. Take 1, 5 or 10 levels, or all of it, from the screen, or pipe it out as liquid XP (20 mB per point).

- **Filter**: choose what to kill: hostile, tamed, adults, entity type and more. Players are never hit unless the server allows it.
- Redstone control is set in the screen.
