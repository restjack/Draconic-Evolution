# Awakened Draconium

**Awakened Draconium** is the upgraded form of Draconium, needed for Draconic-tier gear, injectors and cores, and for the outer shell of a tier 8 [Energy Core](../energy_core/overview.md).

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:decompress/awakened_draconium_ingot" />

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:compress/awakened_draconium_block" />

It cannot be mined. It is made with [Fusion Crafting](../fusion_crafting/overview.md): four **Draconium Blocks** in the core, with six **Draconium Cores** and one **Dragon Heart** in the injectors. The craft needs **Wyvern injectors or better** and 50 million OP, and gives four **Awakened Draconium Blocks**.

## The Dragon Heart

<ItemImage id="draconicevolution:dragon_heart" scale="2" />

Every time the **Ender Dragon** dies, a Dragon Heart appears floating above the exit portal. It does not despawn, so come back for it after the fight.

The [Chaos Guardian](../endgame/chaos_guardian.md) drops one too.

## Using it

One Dragon Heart makes four blocks, which is 36 ingots. Each block splits into nine **Awakened Draconium Ingots**, and each ingot into nine nuggets, the same way as normal Draconium.

Since the dragon can be fought again, every respawn is another heart. Look up **Awakened Draconium** in JEI to see where it is used.
