# The Draconic Reactor

The **Draconic Reactor** turns [Awakened Draconium](../getting_started/awakened_draconium.md) into huge amounts of energy. It is also the most dangerous thing in the mod: if its containment field fails it explodes, and a full-size reactor leaves a crater hundreds of blocks wide.

**Untested in this port.** Upstream also reports odd behaviour, such as the core jumping straight to 2,500 °C on start-up. Build and test it in a copy of the world before you build one on the server.

## Outline

- [Build it](../endgame/reactor_building.md): a core, four Reactor Stabilizers around it and at least one Reactor Energy Injector.
- [Fuel and run it](../endgame/reactor_operation.md): load Awakened Draconium, charge it from a big energy store, activate it, then balance what goes in against what comes out with Flow Gates.
- [Keep it safe](../endgame/reactor_safety.md): know what makes it fail and how to shut it down.

The parts need [Chaos Shards](../endgame/chaos_guardian.md), and you need a large buffer such as an [Energy Core](../energy_core/overview.md).
