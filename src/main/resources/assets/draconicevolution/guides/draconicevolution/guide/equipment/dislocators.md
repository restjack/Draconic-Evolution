# Dislocators

Dislocators are teleporters, and all of them work across dimensions.
- **Dislocator**: one fixed destination, limited uses.
- **Advanced Dislocator**: a list of destinations, fuelled by ender pearls.
- **Bound Dislocators**: go to a matching dislocator or to a player.

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:tools/dislocator" />

For pedestals and portals see [Pedestals and Portals](../equipment/portals.md). For pulling in dropped items see [Item Dislocator](../equipment/item_dislocator.md).

## Dislocator

**Shift-right-click** to save the spot you stand on, the way you face and the dimension. This cannot be changed later. Right-click to teleport there.

Each jump costs one heart, and you need more than one heart left. It has 32 uses and can be repaired with Draconium ingots on an anvil. Left-click a mob to send it there instead; sending players needs the advanced version.

## Advanced Dislocator

**Shift-right-click** opens its screen: up to 256 named destinations. Add where you stand, double-click a name to rename it, drag to reorder, and lock entries so they are not changed by accident.

Each teleport burns one ender pearl of fuel and no health; add pearls with the fuel buttons. Right-click teleports to the selected destination. In blink mode right-click instead jumps up to 32 blocks where you look, four blinks per pearl.

## More tricks

Controls has unbound keys to move the selection, teleport, blink or open the screen without right-clicking.

Left-click a mob to send it to the selected destination. A player can be sent too, but only while they sneak, which counts as their consent.

## Bound Dislocators

Right-click an **Un-Bound Dislocator (Point-to-Point)** to split it into a linked pair. Either one takes you to wherever the other is: on a pedestal, in a receptacle, dropped on the ground or carried by a player.

Right-click an **Un-Bound Dislocator (Player)** to bind it to yourself. Whoever holds it can use it to reach you while you are online. Bound dislocators use no fuel and never wear out.
