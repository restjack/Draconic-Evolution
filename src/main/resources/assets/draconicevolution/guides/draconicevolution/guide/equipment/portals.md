# Pedestals and Portals

A **Dislocator Pedestal** holds a dislocator. Right-click the pedestal to go to the held dislocator's destination; no uses, fuel or health are spent. Right-click it with a dislocator in hand to place one, and shift-right-click to take it back. Wool under the pedestal silences it.

A pedestal makes a good home for one half of a point-to-point [bound dislocator](../equipment/dislocators.md) pair.

## Portals

Build a flat ring of **Infused Obsidian**, any shape, and swap one frame block for a **Dislocator Receptacle** touching the inside of the ring. Insert a dislocator that has a destination and the portal fills in; walk through to travel. Taking the dislocator out closes it.

The default limit is 65,536 portal blocks (about 256x256). Right-click the receptacle with infused obsidian to disguise it.

## Redstone and automation

Without redstone the portal opens whenever a dislocator is inside. A redstone signal also opens it, losing the signal closes it, and while powered the dislocator is locked in. Hoppers and pipes can insert and remove dislocators.

## Energy through portals

Put the two halves of a point-to-point pair into two receptacles. Save an energy crystal to a **Crystal Binder** and right-click a receptacle to link them (one crystal per receptacle, within 32 blocks). Do the same at the other portal.

The two crystals then act as if linked directly, at any distance and across dimensions. Both ends must stay loaded.
