# Energy Relay Crystals

Relay crystals are the hubs of a network. They link only to other crystals (and to portal receptacles), never to machines, but they take many more links than I/O crystals.
- Basic: 8 links
- Wyvern: 16 links
- Draconic: 32 links

Chain relays to cover long distances. Each hop is limited by its tier's range: 32, 64 or 127 blocks.
