# Energy Pylons

Pylons move energy in and out of the core. Place a pylon, then put any glass block on the side that faces the core; the glass turns into the pylon's sphere. The core has to be roughly within 18 blocks on that side.

**Shift-right-click** the pylon to draw a line to its core, or to cycle between cores in range. Pylons do nothing while the core is inactive.

## Input or output

Right-click the sphere to switch modes. The action bar tells you which one you picked:
- **INPUT**: charges the core from whatever feeds the pylon. Green sides.
- **OUTPUT**: drains the core into the blocks around the pylon, including cables. Red sides.

Stained glass colours the pylon's particles. A comparator next to a pylon reads how full the core is.
