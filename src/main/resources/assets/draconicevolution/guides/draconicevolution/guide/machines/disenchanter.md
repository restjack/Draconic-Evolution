# Disenchanter

Moves enchantments from an item onto books, one at a time, for experience levels.

Put the enchanted item in the first slot and plain **Books** in the second, then pick the enchantment to remove. It comes out as an enchanted book; the output slot must be empty.

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:disenchanter" />

Cost per enchantment level: 7 levels for one-level enchantments, 4 for those that max at II, and 3 for the rest. Sharpness V costs 15 levels.

Removing an enchantment also lowers the item's anvil repair cost.
