# Draconium Chest

A large chest with 260 slots, a built-in crafting grid and a five-slot electric furnace.

The furnace runs on energy (1 million OP buffer), supplied by cables or by a charged item in its energy slot. A slider sets the speed. Faster smelting costs much more energy, so turn it down if power is short.

The chest's colour can be changed in its screen.

## The furnace

**Auto feed** moves smeltable items from the chest into the furnace:
- **Off**
- **All**: fills the furnace with anything smeltable.
- **Filter**: only tops up the item types already in the furnace.
- **Sticky filter**: like Filter, but leaves one item in each slot so the filter stays set.

Smelted items go straight into the chest. Shulker boxes, bags and other Draconium Chests cannot be stored in it.
