# Cores

Cores are the crafting parts that set the tier of almost everything in the mod. There are four, from lowest to highest:
- **Draconium Core**
- **Wyvern Core**
- **Draconic Core** (made from Awakened Draconium)
- **Chaotic Core**

Items that hold energy also need an **Energy Controller**: Wyvern, Draconic or Chaotic.

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:components/wyvern_core" />

Four Draconium Cores, Draconium ingots and a Nether Star.

Any of the four cores can also turn a vanilla spawner into a [Stabilized Spawner](../machines/stabilized_spawner.md).

<Recipe id="draconicevolution:components/draconium_core" />

The basic core: Draconium, gold and a diamond. Needed for the Fusion Crafting Core and most early machines.

## Higher tiers

The **Draconic Core** needs Wyvern injectors and [Awakened Draconium](../getting_started/awakened_draconium.md).

The **Chaotic Core** needs Draconic injectors and **Large Chaos Fragments**, which come from the [Chaos Guardian](../endgame/chaos_guardian.md).
