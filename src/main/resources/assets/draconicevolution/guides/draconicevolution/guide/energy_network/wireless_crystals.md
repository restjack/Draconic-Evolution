# Wireless Energy Crystals

A wireless crystal links to other crystals like any crystal does, and also to machines without touching them.
- Basic: 4 crystal links, 16 machines, 32,000 OP/t per machine
- Wyvern: 8 crystal links, 32 machines, 128,000 OP/t
- Draconic: 16 crystal links, 64 machines, 512,000 OP/t

Higher tiers are crafted from the tier below; the Draconic one is in JEI.

Particles show energy moving between the crystal and each machine.

## Linking machines

Save the wireless crystal to a **Crystal Binder**, then right-click a machine on the side the energy should go through. Click the same machine again to unlink it. A side that cannot take or give energy is refused.

**Shift-right-click** the crystal to switch between Output to Block (powers the machines, the default) and Input from Block (pulls from generators or energy stores).
