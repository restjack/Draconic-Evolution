# Setting Up

Place the **Fusion Crafting Core**, then place injectors in line with it: above, below, north, south, east or west. An injector may sit up to one block off that line.


- **Range**: up to 16 blocks from the core.

- **Too close**: an injector within 2 blocks of the core (no more than one empty block between them) stops the whole setup, and the core says an injector is too close. Leave at least two empty blocks.

## Facing and line of sight

- **Facing**: an injector points back towards you when placed, so stand between it and the core. It has to point at the core.
- **Line of sight**: nothing solid may sit between an injector and the core, and neither may another injector. Glass and other see-through blocks are fine.

You can use as many injectors as you like, in any mix of tiers. Open the core after changing the layout; it rescans the injectors.

## Layouts

A common layout is a ring of injectors on a horizontal plane around the core, three or more blocks out, or two rows above and below it pointing down and up.

Power every injector a recipe uses. Each one only takes energy while a craft is charging.
